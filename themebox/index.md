# Privacy Policy — ThemeBox

**Last Updated: October 2026** • **Organization: LuwengStudio**

LuwengStudio is committed to protecting your privacy. This document explains how the application **ThemeBox By LuwengStudio** (`com.luwengstudios.themebox`) handles your information.

---

### 1. No Account Required
ThemeBox requires no account, no sign-in, and no Google authentication. You can browse the catalog and preview all invitation themes without providing any personal information.

### 2. Order Information — Stored On Your Device Only
When you place an order, the details you type into the order form — such as your name, WhatsApp number, event name, event date and time, venue, address, map link, the event owner's bank / e-wallet / QRIS account for the digital envelope, gift shipping address, dress code, live-streaming link, and notes — are stored **exclusively in the app's local database on your device**.

We do not upload, copy, or transmit your order data to our servers or to any third party. The current version has no cloud synchronization.

### 3. WhatsApp Sharing Is Your Choice
Tapping **"Kirim Bukti via WA"** opens WhatsApp with a short pre-filled message containing only your order code. Nothing is sent automatically — you review the message and choose to send it yourself. Anything you share through WhatsApp is governed by WhatsApp's own privacy policy.

### 4. Internet Usage
The app uses internet access for a single purpose: rendering theme previews correctly (e.g., loading font assets inside the built-in preview). This version contains no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK, and no user tracking of any kind.

### 5. Permissions
ThemeBox requests exactly one Android permission:
* **Internet** — required to display theme previews as designed.

### 6. Data Retention & Deletion
Your order history lives only on your device and is permanently erased when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app. For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact us at the address below.

### 7. Children's Privacy
ThemeBox is a general-purpose utility and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

### 8. Changes to This Policy
This policy may be updated as the app evolves (for example, if cloud order synchronization is introduced in a future version). The current version will always be published in this repository.
