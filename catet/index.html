  • Data yang Diproses: Kami hanya menerima data profil publik dasar terverifikasi, meliputi Nama Lengkap, Alamat Email terdaftar, ID Pengguna Unik (UID), dan Foto Profil.

3 Penggunaan Data Lokasi & Telematika GPS

Aplikasi CATET meminta izin lokasi presisi (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION dan ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION) secara transparan untuk utilitas utama yang diminta secara sadar oleh pengguna:

  • Radar BBM (Pencarian SPBU Terdekat): Koordinat GPS perangkat digunakan secara real-time untuk menghitung jarak langsung ke stasiun SPBU Pertamina terdekat di sekitar posisi pengguna dan menampilkan panduan rute navigasi. Koordinat ini diproses secara lokal di memori kerja dan tidak disimpan sebagai jejak lokasi di server mana pun.
  • Mode Mengemudi & Telematika Kecepatan: Menggunakan sensor Doppler GNSS perangkat untuk menampilkan kecepatan real-time (Speedometer) dan menghitung akumulasi jarak tempuh harian (KM) guna menentukan efisiensi konsumsi bahan bakar (KM/Liter) serta pengingat servis kendaraan berkala.

CATET tidak melakukan pelacakan lokasi di latar belakang ketika fitur mengemudi atau radar BBM tidak aktif.

Layanan dapat Anda hentikan seketika melalui tombol "Hentikan" di notifikasi.

  • Kepatuhan Scoped Storage: CATET tidak meminta izin akses penyimpanan luas (MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE). Ekspor manual berkas cadangan dilakukan melalui Storage Access Framework (SAF) resmi Android.

1 Core Principles & LuwengStudio Commitment

LuwengStudio fully respects your privacy and personal data protection rights. The CATET application is built on the foundations of Privacy-by-Design and Offline-First Local Storage. We ensure that your financial logs, business cashflow calculations, daily trip histories, and operational data remain strictly under your own control on your local device storage.

Zero Data Commercialization Guarantee: LuwengStudio has never and will never sell, lease, share, or trade your personal data, financial records, or location coordinates with advertisers, data brokers, or any third parties.

2 Google Identity Authentication

To verify account ownership and provide seamless, secure cloud backups, CATET utilizes Google Identity & Firebase Authentication:

  • Processed Data: We only receive verified basic public profile information, including Full Name, Registered Email Address, Unique User ID (UID), and Profile Photo.
  • Processing Purpose: Used for identity verification, unauthorized access prevention, and backup ownership validation. We never request, access, or store your Google account password.

3 Location Data & GPS Telematics

The CATET application transparently requests precise location permissions (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION and ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION) strictly for core utilities consciously triggered by the user:

  • Fuel Radar (Nearest Gas Stations): Device GPS coordinates are used in real-time to compute distance to nearby official Pertamina gas stations and provide route guidance. Coordinates are processed locally in volatile memory and are never stored as location trails on any server.
  • Driving Mode & Speedometer Telematics: Utilizes device GNSS Doppler velocity to deliver instantaneous real-time speed measurements and calculate accumulated daily trip distance (KM) for fuel efficiency tracking (KM/Liter) and routine vehicle maintenance alerts.

CATET never performs background location tracking when driving mode or fuel radar features are inactive.

4 Background Services & WorkManager

The application complies with Android power management and modern Google Play background execution policies:

  • Foreground Service with Persistent Notification: While Driving Mode or the Floating Speedometer Widget is active, the system runs a foreground service with a persistent notification in the status bar so you can accurately monitor distance and speed while using external navigation apps. You can terminate the service at any moment via the "Stop" action button.
  • Scheduled Backups via Jetpack WorkManager: Automatic backup tasks utilize battery-optimized Android Jetpack WorkManager, adhering to system constraints without requiring aggressive exact alarm permissions.

5 Google Drive AppData Cloud Backup & Encryption

CATET implements state-of-the-art cloud backup security standards:

  • Google Drive AppData Folder: Automated cloud backups are stored directly inside a private, hidden application partition (appDataFolder) within the user's personal Google Drive account. LuwengStudio developers have zero access to view, inspect, or manage any of your personal Google Drive content outside of CATET's dedicated backup file.
  • Military-Grade Encryption (AES-256-GCM): All backup files (.ctt) are compressed with GZIP and locally encrypted in device memory using AES-256-GCM prior to transmission.
  • Scoped Storage Compliance: CATET does not request broad external storage access (MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE). Manual file exports operate via Android's official Storage Access Framework (SAF).

6 User Rights & Data Deletion

In accordance with Google Play Developer Policy, GDPR (Article 17 Right to Erasure), and global data protection regulations:

  • In-App Immediate Deletion: You can wipe all transaction records, trip histories, and local data instantly through the Reset All Data option in the CATET settings menu.
  • Account Erasure Requests: You may request permanent deletion of your profile identity from our systems by contacting our privacy desk at luwengstudio@gmail.com or via our official Telegram channel. Requests are processed within 3 business days.

7 Third-Party Service Providers

We only collaborate with trusted infrastructure partners compliant with international security standards:

  • Google Play Services: High-precision Fused Location Provider and Google Drive API services.
  • Google Firebase: Secure authentication gateway (Google Privacy Policy).
  • OpenStreetMap (OSM) & Fuel APIs: Public directory datasets for gas station coordinates and official fuel pricing.
